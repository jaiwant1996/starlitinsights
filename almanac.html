Put it in your calendar

Downloads a standard .ics file. Open it and every date below lands in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook as an all-day entry.

Dates are computed from the same ephemeris used for your reading — JPL orbital elements for the planets, the full Meeus lunar theory for the Moon, and the Lahiri ayanamsha. Times shown are in your birth time zone. A date is exact; what it means for you is not something a calendar can tell you.
