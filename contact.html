What to write to us about

  • Booking help — trouble finding a slot, or needing a time outside those listed.
  • Birth details — corrections, or questions about an uncertain birth time.
  • Payments and refunds — anything covered by our Cancellation and Refunds policy.
  • Your data — access, correction or deletion requests under our Privacy Policy.
  • Corrections — anything on this site you think we got wrong. Genuinely welcome.

Before you write about a reading

If your question is about what a placement or period means, the fastest route is to book a consultation rather than exchange emails — a chart cannot responsibly be read in a message thread without full birth data.

Grievance

If you are unhappy with any aspect of the service, or with how your data has been handled, write to starlitinsights24@gmail.com with “Grievance” in the subject line. We acknowledge within 2 business days and aim to resolve the matter within 30 days.
